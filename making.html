Playable Development Iterations

Iteration 1 · Foundation git 9096342

Gravity Engine & Core Roda (17 Moves)

↗ Play v1 Fullscreen

Roda Orbital started as a 60 Hz deterministic fixed-point Capoeira fighting engine built around three stances (GINGA, HSP Bananeira, RLX Negativa), high/low/throw crush mechanics, and Moon 0.165g low-gravity air juggles with full iPad touch input.

  • 21-bone forward kinematics skeleton with swept bone-capsule hit detection.
  • 17 foundational techniques: Ginga, Ponteira, Bênção, Compasso, Aú Batido, Rasteira, Pião.
  • On-screen iPad thumbstick + 5-button arcade pad alongside desktop keyboard controls.
  • Live wireframe hitbox/hurtbox toggle (F3) & Lunar 0.165g orbit arena.
🌙 Preset: Lunar 0.165g · Hitboxes ON · Faísca vs Touro
Iteration 2 · Arcade Visuals & IK git 94e729d

SF2 Cel-Shading, 2-Bone IK & Full 10-Fighter Roster

↗ Play v2 Fullscreen

To bring arcade fighting clarity to Capoeira's swirling inversions, Iteration 2 introduced custom 4-band Street Fighter II toon lighting, ink outlines, 2-bone foot & palm ground IK pinning, Verlet spring ribbons, and 10 distinct fighters.

  • Continuous 4-bone skinned torso, deltoids, arms, and draped cotton abadá trousers.
  • Analytical 2-bone IK pinning grounded feet and Bananeira palms cleanly to y = 0.
  • Starburst hit flashes, limb smear trails, hitstop stun shakes, and CPS-1 Arcade CRT shader.
📺 Preset: CPS-1 CRT Filter · Bahia Sunset · Faísca vs Morcego
Iteration 3 · Encyclopedia git 413561c

3D Move Showcase & 114-Technique Library (+97 Moves)

↗ Open 3D Showcase

Iteration 3 integrated 97 additional moves—completing 100% 1-to-1 coverage of both Mudroljub's catalog (90 active / 93 total) and Minhoquinho's 52 movement videos—inside an interactive 360° 3D Move Animator with single-frame scrubbing and training lab support.

  • Every Aú variation, Escorpião kick, Queda de Rins transition, Tesoura, and Mortal somersault.
  • Interactive 360° orbit stage, frame scrubber, slow-motion speed control, and auto-tour.
  • Training Arena link (v3 Training) lets you chain all moves live with hitboxes visible.
🕺 Preset: Interactive 3D Move Animator & Dummy Partner
Iteration 4 · Culture & Duet git db8f50d

Jogar na Roda (Play / Show Mode), Tutorial & History

↗ Play v4 Fullscreen

Capturing the essence of Capoeira's cooperative circle, Iteration 4 built Play / Show Mode: partners exchange synchronized kicks and duck/cartwheel replies with zero damage while building the shared Axé streak meter—paired with our Tutorial & History guides.

  • Pergunta & Resposta choreographed dialogue AI across 18 classic sequences and move-specific counters.
  • Zero-KO cooperative mode with instant F1 mid-duet human takeover.
  • Published dedicated How to Play manual and History of Capoeira heritage page.
🤝 Preset: Play / Show Duet · Toque Angola · Faísca vs Mestre Jacaré
Iteration 5 · Latest Release git main (head)

Biomechanics, Style Flow, Skins, Filters & 3D Visual Overhaul

↗ Play v5 Fullscreen

Our latest release deepens martial movement and visual craft: counter-twist torso biomechanics, Angola vs Regional AI personalities (+ Chamada & Volta ao Mundo rituals = 116 moves), 5 stage aesthetics, 7 skins, 6 post-processing filters, and a full 3-tier graphics upgrade.

  • Added Angola rituals Chamada & Volta ao Mundo (+2 moves) and 36 flowing cancel routes.
  • Selectable Berimbau Toques (Angola, São Bento Grande, Iúna), Lunar gravity & P2 controls in Play Mode.
  • Weighted socket outlines (zero shoulder dots), 16-strip Verlet Maculelê grass skirt, sculpted faces/boots.
  • Spherical Lunar Earthrise, Salvador Pelourinho skyline, Sumi-e mountains, Noturna lights & limb AO puddles.
