How to Play Roda Orbital

Roda Orbital is a deterministic 60 FPS 3D Capoeira fighting game built around fluid stance switching, rhythm-synced timing, high/low crush physics, and a full library of 114 authentic Capoeira techniques — from competitive bouts to collaborative Jogar na Roda display play.

2. Play / Show Mode — Jogar na Roda

Supportive Synchronized Display

Traditional Capoeira is called jogo ("the game") because skilled players converse with kicks and escapes rather than brute-force brawling. Activating 🤝 Play / Show transforms the match into an unbroken choreographic exhibition.

  • Zero Damage & Mutual Escape: Attacks never knock down or drain health. Whenever a technique clears its partner cleanly at close range, both players build the golden Harmony Flow gauge and extend their duet streak.
  • Pergunta e Resposta (Call & Answer): High spinning kicks such as Armada or Queixada prompt timely ducks (Cocorinha, Negativa, Esquiva), while low sweeps trigger acrobatic vaults (Aú, Macaco, Salto Mortal).
  • Jump Into the Duet: Both capoeiristas dance together automatically at first. Press F1 (or click P1 Auto) to take over Player 1—whenever you launch an attack near your partner, the Show AI answers with a matching Capoeira dodge synchronized to pass cleanly underneath or over your strike.

4. The Three Stances & Crush Triangle

Unlike static fighting-game crouches, Capoeira posture defines both your attack list and which parts of your body can be hit. Hold back relative to your opponent while upright in GINGA to block standing strikes, or shift into RLX / HSP to bypass entire height levels cleanly.

Balanced · Neutral

GINGA (Upright Rhythm)

Your default swaying footwork. Grants fast stepping speed, standing/mid guard while holding back, and direct access to core kicks (Ponteira, Martelo, Queixada, Armada) plus throws.

CRUSH_HIGH · Floor

RLX (Negativa)

Enter directly with 2+O (S+O). Drops your head and upper torso near the ground so HIGH kicks sail overhead while loading low sweeps (Rasteira) and rising strikes (Chapa de Frente, Cabeçada).

CRUSH_LOW · Inverted

HSP (Bananeira)

Enter directly with 8+O (W+O). Lifts both legs skyward atop planted hands, letting low ground attacks miss underneath while unlocking spinning aerials (Helicóptero, Pião de Mão, Parafuso).

5. Command Notation & Signature Techniques

Move lists use standard directional numpad notation facing your opponent: 6 means Toward, 4 means Away, 2 means Down, 8 means Up, and 236 is a smooth quarter-circle sweep toward your partner.

6. Bateria Rhythm Sync, Malícia & Maculelê

~105.9 BPM Beat Clock

Downbeat Timing Window

Watch the pulsing berimbau ring beneath the match timer. Launching attacks or shifting stances within ±4 frames of a downbeat grants:

  • −2 frames faster stance-shift recovery (minimum 4f)
  • +1 frame head start on move startup
  • +5 Malícia meter gain per synced action
Dual-Machete Tradition

Maculelê Costume & Retro CRT

Toggle P1 Maculelê ⚔️ on the toolbar to equip your fighter with twin machetes (facões) for rhythmic dance-combat flair, or enable 📺 Retro CRT to switch on arcade scanline and street-fighter palette styling.

7. Playable Fighter Archetypes

All ten fighters share the full 114-move Capoeira vocabulary while expressing distinct body scales, ginga tempos, stance-shift speeds, and damage multipliers.

Regional

Faísca · Touro · Ciclone

Faísca leads fast aerial juggles (170 HP); Touro anchors heavy armored contragolpe power (190 HP, 1.15× dmg); Ciclone excels at long-reach whirling kicks.

Angola

Velho Jacaré · Sombra

Measured ginga cadences steeped in floor deception (malícia), razor-sharp low sweeps, and counter-mestre baits.

Contemporânea & Maculelê

Morcego · Quebra · Grima · Órbita · Pipoca

Morcego boasts an instant 4-frame Bananeira shift; Quebra brings breaker-style freezes; Grima embodies Maculelê rhythm; Órbita sports a zero-G suit; Pipoca brings the smallest hurtbox and lightning walks.

8. Quick Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some attacks pass straight over my crouched fighter or under a handstand?

Hit detection checks actual 3D bone capsules rather than a single rectangle box. In RLX (Negativa), upper crush overrides ignore HIGH attacks targeting head height; in HSP (Bananeira) or airborne cartwheels, lifted legs clear incoming LOW sweeps cleanly.

How do I practice jumping into Play / Show Mode on my own?

Click 🤝 Play / Show on the HUD toolbar, watch P1 and P2 converse across a couple of Pergunta e Resposta sequences, then press F1 (or tap P1 Auto) to take manual control of P1. Throw any kick or sweep near your partner and watch them answer automatically!

Does Roda Orbital work on an iPad in Safari or Chrome?

Yes! Touch-capable browsers automatically reveal the on-screen thumbstick and five action buttons (LH, RH, LK, RK, STANCE). All four pages also respect safe-area screen insets and container-width responsive cards on portrait tablets and phones.

How can I verify every special command and compare with external Capoeira catalogs?

Visit the 3D Move Showcase to scrub individual frames, inspect active hit capsules, preview paired call-and-response dodges, and open the full Mudroljub & Minhoquinho 1-to-1 coverage matrix.
